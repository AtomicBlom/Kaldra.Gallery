# Kenney assets

These are from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s asset packs, released under
[Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): free to use, change and
redistribute, with no attribution required. They are credited here as thanks.

| Folder | Pack |
|---|---|
| `castle/` | [Castle Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit): the wall, tower, stairs, rock and tree pieces and their colour map |
| `characters/` | [Animated Characters: Survivors](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-survivors): `characterMedium`, merged with its animations into one glTF by `tools/convert_character_fbx.py` |
| `dungeon/` | [Tiny Dungeon](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon): the packed tile map |

The gallery's other assets (`brick_albedo.png`, `brick_normal.png`, `cube.gltf`, `rigged_bar.glb`) were made for this
repository and are under its MIT licence.
